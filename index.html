About

The person behind the code

I'm Puspa — a developer, designer and editor from the hills of Kavre, Nepal. My work sits at the crossing point of creativity and technical precision: I write the code that makes an interface function, and I shape the visuals that make it feel considered.

Across web design, graphic design and video editing, the throughline is the same — take a rough idea and give it a clean, confident shape. I'm equally comfortable structuring a database as I am laying out a logo or cutting a reel, and I bring that range to every project I take on.

Currently focused on sharpening my full-stack craft and taking on projects that push past what I built last time.
